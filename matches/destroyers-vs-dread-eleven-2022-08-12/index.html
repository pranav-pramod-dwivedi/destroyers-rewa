EDITORIAL MATCH REPORT By RDCA Senior Cricket Correspondent
12 Aug 2022 • 5 Min Read

Dread Eleven Edge Dramatic Derby Encounter: Match Tactical Analysis at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium

Competing under the prestigious banner of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Dread Eleven seized a memorable victory (Dread Eleven won by 21 runs) on 12 Aug 2022 at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium.

The 2022 encounter at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium commenced with a high-stakes toss won by Destroyers Cricket Club, electing to elected to bat first. On a lively APSU turf carrying genuine pace and consistent bounce, Destroyers Cricket Club opened proceedings with 162/6 in 20 overs. Pranav Dwivedi anchored the top order with 68 off 29 balls, while Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered through Rohit Rajawat (2/9 in 4 overs).

In the second innings, Dread Eleven replied with a spirited performance of 183/10 across 20 overs. Kuldeep Sen spearheaded the charge with 46 runs off 30 deliveries. The bowling unit maintained relentless pressure through Harsh Gawli's incisive spell of 4/66, keeping boundaries strictly curtailed in the crucial middle overs.

Tactically, captain Yash Dubey displayed brilliant game awareness, implementing restrictive ring field placements during overs 10 to 18 and utilizing reverse-swing yorkers at the death to starve the batting side of scoring momentum.

The decisive honours were awarded to Kuldeep Sen as Player of the Match for 46 runs off 30 balls. This marquee clash added another legendary installment to the storied rivalry between Destroyers and Dread Eleven in the Rewa Division Cricket Association calendar.

Tactical Post-Mortem

Turning Point

The disciplined bowling squeeze from Rohit Rajawat and calculated finishing by Kuldeep Sen.

Pitch & Conditions

APSU Stadium wicket provided authentic carry and bounce, rewarding disciplined hit-the-deck pace bowling.

Captain's Masterstroke

Capt. Yash Dubey rotated spin in short bursts, unsettling Destroyers' middle-order timing.

Player of the Match

Kuldeep Sen: 46 runs off 30 balls
