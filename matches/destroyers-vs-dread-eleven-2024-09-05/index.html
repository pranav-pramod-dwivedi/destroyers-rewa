EDITORIAL MATCH REPORT By RDCA Senior Cricket Correspondent
05 Sep 2024 • 5 Min Read

Dread Eleven Edge Dramatic Derby Encounter: Match Tactical Analysis at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium

Competing under the prestigious banner of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Dread Eleven seized a memorable victory (Dread Eleven won by 1 wickets) on 05 Sep 2024 at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium.

The 2024 encounter at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium commenced with a high-stakes toss won by Dread Eleven, electing to elected to bat first. On a lively APSU turf carrying genuine pace and consistent bounce, Destroyers Cricket Club opened proceedings with 264/9 in 50 overs. Kanishk Dubey anchored the top order with 51 off 59 balls, while Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered through Rahul Batham (3/91 in 9 overs).

In the second innings, Dread Eleven replied with a spirited performance of 266/9 across 50 overs. Akhil Mishra spearheaded the charge with 69 runs off 75 deliveries. The bowling unit maintained relentless pressure through Pranav Dwivedi's incisive spell of 3/56, keeping boundaries strictly curtailed in the crucial middle overs.

Tactically, captain Akhil Mishra displayed brilliant game awareness, implementing restrictive ring field placements during overs 10 to 18 and utilizing reverse-swing yorkers at the death to starve the batting side of scoring momentum.

The decisive honours were awarded to Rahul Batham as Player of the Match for 3/91 in 9 ov. This marquee clash added another legendary installment to the storied rivalry between Destroyers and Dread Eleven in the Rewa Division Cricket Association calendar.

The disciplined bowling squeeze from Rahul Batham and calculated finishing by Rahul Batham.
