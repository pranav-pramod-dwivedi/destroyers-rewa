EDITORIAL MATCH REPORT By RDCA Senior Cricket Correspondent
15 Aug 2021 • 5 Min Read

Dread Eleven Edge Dramatic Derby Encounter: Match Tactical Analysis at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium

Competing under the prestigious banner of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Dread Eleven seized a memorable victory (Dread Eleven won by 5 wickets) on 15 Aug 2021 at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium.

The 2021 encounter at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium commenced with a high-stakes toss won by Destroyers Cricket Club, electing to elected to bat first. On a lively APSU turf carrying genuine pace and consistent bounce, Destroyers Cricket Club opened proceedings with 151/8 in 20 overs. Atharv Mahajan anchored the top order with 36 off 23 balls, while Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered through Rajat Patidar (3/72 in 4 overs).

In the second innings, Dread Eleven replied with a spirited performance of 181/5 across 20 overs. Subhranshu Senapati spearheaded the charge with 70 runs off 46 deliveries. The bowling unit maintained relentless pressure through Yash Dubey's incisive spell of 2/34, keeping boundaries strictly curtailed in the crucial middle overs.

Tactically, captain Akhil Mishra displayed brilliant game awareness, implementing restrictive ring field placements during overs 10 to 18 and utilizing reverse-swing yorkers at the death to starve the batting side of scoring momentum.

The decisive honours were awarded to Rajat Patidar as Player of the Match for 3/72 in 4 ov. This marquee clash added another legendary installment to the storied rivalry between Destroyers and Dread Eleven in the Rewa Division Cricket Association calendar.

Tactical Post-Mortem

Turning Point

The disciplined bowling squeeze from Rajat Patidar and calculated finishing by Rajat Patidar.

Pitch & Conditions

APSU Stadium wicket provided authentic carry and bounce, rewarding disciplined hit-the-deck pace bowling.

Captain's Masterstroke

Capt. Akhil Mishra rotated spin in short bursts, unsettling Destroyers' middle-order timing.
