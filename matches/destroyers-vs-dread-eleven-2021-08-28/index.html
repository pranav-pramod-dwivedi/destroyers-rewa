EDITORIAL MATCH REPORT By RDCA Senior Cricket Correspondent
28 Aug 2021 • 5 Min Read

Destroyers Roar to Derby Glory: Tactical Breakdown of Rewa Triumph at Martand School Ground No. 3

Competing under the prestigious banner of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Destroyers Cricket Club seized a memorable victory (Destroyers won by 14 runs) on 28 Aug 2021 at Martand School Ground No. 3.

The 2021 encounter at Martand School Ground No. 3 commenced with a high-stakes toss won by Destroyers Cricket Club, electing to elected to bowl first. On a tested Martand Ground No. 3 surface offering early seam movement and turn for finger spinners, Destroyers Cricket Club opened proceedings with 183/10 in 20 overs. Pranav Dwivedi anchored the top order with 53 off 23 balls, while Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered through Ashwin Das (6/92 in 4 overs).

In the second innings, Dread Eleven replied with a spirited performance of 169/5 across 20 overs. Ramveer Singh Gurjar spearheaded the charge with 62 runs off 40 deliveries. The bowling unit maintained relentless pressure through Pranav Dwivedi's incisive spell of 2/21, keeping boundaries strictly curtailed in the crucial middle overs.

Tactically, captain Pranav Dwivedi displayed brilliant game awareness, implementing restrictive ring field placements during overs 10 to 18 and utilizing reverse-swing yorkers at the death to starve the batting side of scoring momentum.

The decisive honours were awarded to Pranav Dwivedi as Player of the Match for 53 runs off 23 balls. This marquee clash added another legendary installment to the storied rivalry between Destroyers and Dread Eleven in the Rewa Division Cricket Association calendar.

Tactical Post-Mortem

Turning Point

The crucial dismissal of Pranav Dwivedi and death-overs yorker execution by Pranav Dwivedi.

Pitch & Conditions

Martand Ground No. 3 surface rewarded disciplined lines, yielding assistance to spinners and reverse-swing in the afternoon.

Captain's Masterstroke

Capt. Pranav Dwivedi choked the cover boundary with defensive sweepers, forcing aerial miscues in the death overs.

Player of the Match

Pranav Dwivedi: 53 runs off 23 balls
