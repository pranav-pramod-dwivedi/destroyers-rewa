EDITORIAL MATCH REPORT By RDCA Senior Cricket Correspondent
12 Sep 2025 • 5 Min Read

Destroyers Roar to Derby Glory: Tactical Breakdown of Rewa Triumph at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium

Competing under the prestigious banner of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament, Destroyers Cricket Club seized a memorable victory (Destroyers won by 45 runs) on 12 Sep 2025 at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium.

The 2025 encounter at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium commenced with a high-stakes toss won by Destroyers Cricket Club, electing to elected to bat first. On a lively APSU turf carrying genuine pace and consistent bounce, Destroyers Cricket Club opened proceedings with 292/7 in 50 overs. Venkatesh Iyer anchored the top order with 124 off 104 balls, while Dread Eleven's bowling attack countered through Kuldeep Sen (3/52 in 10 overs).

In the second innings, Dread Eleven replied with a spirited performance of 247/10 across 47.4 overs. Yash Dubey spearheaded the charge with 82 runs off 88 deliveries. The bowling unit maintained relentless pressure through Kulwant Khejroliya's incisive spell of 4/44, keeping boundaries strictly curtailed in the crucial middle overs.

Tactically, captain Aryan Deshmukh displayed brilliant game awareness, implementing restrictive ring field placements during overs 10 to 18 and utilizing reverse-swing yorkers at the death to starve the batting side of scoring momentum.

The decisive honours were awarded to Venkatesh Iyer as Player of the Match for 124 (104) & 1/32 (8 ov). This marquee clash added another legendary installment to the storied rivalry between Destroyers and Dread Eleven in the Rewa Division Cricket Association calendar.

Tactical Post-Mortem

Turning Point

The crucial dismissal of Venkatesh Iyer and death-overs yorker execution by Kulwant Khejroliya.

Pitch & Conditions

APSU Stadium wicket provided authentic carry and bounce, rewarding disciplined hit-the-deck pace bowling.

Captain's Masterstroke

Capt. Aryan Deshmukh choked the cover boundary with defensive sweepers, forcing aerial miscues in the death overs.

Player of the Match

Venkatesh Iyer: 124 (104) & 1/32 (8 ov)
