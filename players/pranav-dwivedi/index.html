Pranav Dwivedi: Franchise Captain & Premier All-Rounder

Pranav Dwivedi (Pranav Pramod Dwivedi) is an Indian cricketer and the 3-time consecutive championship-winning captain of Destroyers Cricket Club (DES) in the prestigious Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament in Rewa, Madhya Pradesh. Playing as a premier all-rounder, Dwivedi has amassed 1,435 tournament runs at an average of 57.4 (strike rate 146.4) and seized 66 wickets with an average of 16.3 and best bowling figures of 8/39 across 27 clashes against arch-rivals Dread Eleven.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pranav Dwivedi

Verified answers compiled from official RDCA scorecards, tournament registries, and Destroyers franchise telemetry.

Who is Pranav Dwivedi in Rewa cricket?

Pranav Dwivedi (Pranav Pramod Dwivedi) is the franchise captain and star all-rounder for Destroyers Cricket Club (DES) based in Rewa, Madhya Pradesh. He has guided the team to three consecutive Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament championship titles (2024, 2025, and 2026).

What are Pranav Dwivedi's career batting and bowling statistics?

In tournament play against arch-rivals Dread Eleven, Pranav has registered 1,435 runs in 27 matches with an extraordinary batting average of 57.4 and a strike rate of 146.4 (including 1 century, high score 102*, and 14 fifties). With the ball, he has claimed 66 wickets at an average of 16.3 and an economy rate of 5.48, including career-best figures of 8/39.

What is Pranav Dwivedi's captaincy record in the Rewa Derby?

Pranav Dwivedi has captained Destroyers to 19 derby victories against Akhil Mishra's Dread Eleven across 34 tournament clashes. Under his stewardship, Destroyers secured the 2024 title (4–1), completed a historic 5–0 clean sweep in 2025, and retained their crown in the thrilling 2026 finale by 12 runs (3–2 series).

What is Pranav Dwivedi's playing role and jersey number?

Dwivedi wears Jersey #7 for Destroyers. He operates as an explosive middle-order right-handed batsman capable of accelerating at the death, combined with versatile bowling skills featuring both right-arm fast-medium seam and deceptive off-spin variations.
