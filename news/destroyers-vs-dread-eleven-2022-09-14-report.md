# Derby thriller: Subhranshu Senapati's 7/69 in 7 ov seals it for Dread Eleven

 By **RDCA Media Cell**
 •
 Press Officer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Dread Eleven won by 1 wickets in a 50 Overs derby at Martand School Ground No. 3, Rewa on 14 Sep 2022. Destroyers won the toss and elected to bowl first.

**Subhranshu Senapati** (Dread Eleven) took player-of-the-match honours for **7/69 in 7 ov**. Rohit Rajawat (83 off 95) provided the key support for Dread Eleven. With the ball, Mohd Arham Aquil (1/11 in 8 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 231/9 (50 ov) against DE 261/9 (50 ov). The decisive passage said it all: ‘Short, wide outside off. Slapped violently through backward point! The ropes take a beating as Dread Eleven seal the historic derby triumph in front of a roaring crowd!’ (19.6, Akhil Mishra vs Kulwant Khejroliya).

The result moves the 2022 series to Destroyers 2–4 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 18 Sep 2022.
