# Derby thriller: Mohd Arham Aquil's 5/95 in 9 ov seals it for Dread Eleven

 By **Rewa Cricket Weekly**
 •
 Staff Writer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Dread Eleven won by 2 wickets in a 50 Overs derby at Martand School Ground No. 3, Rewa on 4 Sep 2022. Dread Eleven won the toss and elected to bowl first.

**Mohd Arham Aquil** (Dread Eleven) took player-of-the-match honours for **5/95 in 9 ov**. Akhil Mishra (58 off 60) provided the key support for Dread Eleven. With the ball, Avesh Khan (2/21 in 9 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 232/9 (50 ov) against DE 234/8 (50 ov). The decisive passage said it all: ‘Short, wide outside off. Slapped violently through backward point! The ropes take a beating as Dread Eleven seal the historic derby triumph in front of a roaring crowd!’ (19.6, Akhil Mishra vs Kulwant Khejroliya).

The result moves the 2022 series to Destroyers 0–3 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 7 Sep 2022.
