# How Destroyers Trials Work: Earning the Derby Shirt

 By **RDCA Media Cell**
 •
 Press Officer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Destroyers Cricket Club assembles an expanded pre-season training camp — most recently a 48-player group at APSU Stadium — ahead of each Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament cycle.

Trialists are assessed across formats with emphasis on derby temperament: strike rotation under pressure, death bowling execution and fielding intensity. Performance in local tournament cricket, alongside camp form, feeds final squad selection.

Trial windows and contact points are published through the club's contact desk before each season. Unsuccessful trialists are encouraged to reappear the following cycle.
