# Venkatesh Iyer's 68 (38) &amp; 2/20 (4 ov) fires Destroyers to 22-run win in T20 derby

 By **Rewa Cricket Weekly**
 •
 Staff Writer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Destroyers won by 22 runs in a T20 derby at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium, Rewa on 5 Sep 2025. Destroyers won the toss and elected to bat first.

**Venkatesh Iyer** (Destroyers) took player-of-the-match honours for **68 (38) & 2/20 (4 ov)**. Ajay Rohera (34 off 22) provided the key support for Destroyers. With the ball, Kulwant Khejroliya (3/26 in 4 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 186/5 (20 ov) against DE 164/7 (20 ov).

The result moves the 2025 series to Destroyers 1–0 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 8 Sep 2025.
