Dominant 2024 Campaign: How Destroyers Outplayed Dread Eleven 4–1 in 50-Over Series

By Vindhya Cricket Archive • Senior Analyst

The 2024 season of the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament marked a watershed moment for Destroyers Cricket Club, as they recorded a dominant 4–1 series conquest over Dread Eleven at APSU Stadium.

After dropping the opener by a solitary wicket, Destroyers rallied in spectacular fashion: winning by 5 runs on 8 September (257/6 vs 252/7), edging a thriller by 2 runs on 12 September (276 vs 274), cruising by 18 runs on 16 September (288/6 vs 270), and sealing the finale on 20 September with an 8-run triumph (233/6 vs 225).

Captain Pranav Dwivedi stood tall throughout the campaign, delivering key middle-overs breakthroughs and anchoring crucial partnerships alongside Aryan Deshmukh and Shivam Shukla.
