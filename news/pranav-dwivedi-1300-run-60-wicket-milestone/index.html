All-Round Historic Milestone: Pranav Dwivedi Reaches 1,300 Runs & 60 Wickets in Derby Clashes

By RDCA Sports Desk • Statistician

Destroyers captain Pranav Dwivedi has solidified his standing as the tournament’s most decorated competitor, surpassing 1,340 runs and 60 wickets across all 24 clashes against Dread Eleven.

Averaging 55.9 per innings with 13 half-centuries and a hundred to his name, Dwivedi has combined lethal top-order strokeplay with match-winning bowling spells (best bowling of 8/39). His endurance across all 24 encounters remains unmatched in Rewa division cricket history.

"Every run and every wicket is for Destroyers," Dwivedi remarked after concluding the 2024 season. "We have elevated this franchise to new heights, and our sights are firmly fixed on the 2025 and 2026 seasons."
