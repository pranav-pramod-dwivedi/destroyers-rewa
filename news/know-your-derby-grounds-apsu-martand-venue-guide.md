# Know Your Derby Grounds: APSU Stadium and Martand No. 3 Venue Guide

 By **RDCA Media Cell**
 •
 Press Officer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Every derby in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament is staged at one of two official grounds: the Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium and Martand School Ground No. 3, both in Rewa.

APSU Stadium hosts the marquee fixtures including the championship finals, while Martand No. 3 has staged several low-scoring thrillers decided in the final overs. Gates, parking and entry follow RDCA match-day advisories issued before each game.

Supporters travelling for derby week should check the fixtures page for venue confirmation, as late ground changes are announced via the official news feed.
