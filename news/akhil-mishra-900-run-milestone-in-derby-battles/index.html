Milestone: Akhil Mishra Becomes First Destroyer to Cross 900 Runs Against Dread Eleven

By RDCA Sports Desk • Statistician

With an average of 47.63 and 9 half-centuries across 19 innings, Destroyers skipper Akhil Mishra has etched his name into the annals of Rewa cricket as the franchise’s premier run accumulator.

Mishra reached the 900-run plateau during the 2024 50-Over series, maintaining an exceptional strike rate of 107.6 across both T20 and ODI formats. His calm temperament at the crease has repeatedly steadied Destroyers during high-pressure chases.

"Records are satisfying, but victories for Destroyers are all that matter," said Mishra. "We look forward to raising the bar even higher in the 2025 and 2026 fixtures."
