RDCA Unveils Upgraded Turf Pitches at APSU Stadium for 2025–2026 Cycle

By RDCA Media Cell • Press Officer

Rewa — The Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) has officially concluded extensive rejuvenation work across the central pitch square at APSU Stadium, ensuring international-standard bounce and seam movement for the 2025 and 2026 seasons.

Chief Curator Rajesh Patel confirmed that local red soil combined with clay blends from the Narmada basin was utilized to reinforce the wickets against weathering while ensuring equitable contest between bat and ball.

"Both Destroyers and Dread Eleven showcase fast-paced, modern cricket. These revamped surfaces will reward authentic strokeplay while granting genuine pace bowlers true carry through to the wicketkeeper," Patel stated.
