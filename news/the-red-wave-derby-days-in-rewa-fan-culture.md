# The Red Wave: What Derby Days Feel Like in Rewa

 By **Rewa Cricket Weekly**
 •
 Staff Writer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Ask anyone in Rewa what derby week means and the answer is the same: the town splits in two. Destroyers' red against Dread Eleven's colours, banners up at APSU Stadium and arguments in every tea shop.

The rivalry has produced everything from one-wicket finishes to a tied-run thriller and a 5–0 clean sweep, which is why travelling supporters plan derby week like a festival. Early arrival is advised — the popular stands fill well before the toss.

Win or lose, the derby handshake at full time has become its own tradition, and the return fixture is never more than a season away.
