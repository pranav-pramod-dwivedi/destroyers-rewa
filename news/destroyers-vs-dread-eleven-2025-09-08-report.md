# Aryan Deshmukh's 3/18 (4 ov) &amp; 24* (12) lifts Destroyers to 6-wicket win in T20 derby

 By **RDCA Sports Desk**
 •
 Match Reporter

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Destroyers won by 6 wickets in a T20 derby at Martand School Ground No. 3, Rewa on 8 Sep 2025. Dread Eleven won the toss and elected to bat first.

**Aryan Deshmukh** (Destroyers) took player-of-the-match honours for **3/18 (4 ov) & 24* (12)**. Ajay Rohera (52 off 38) provided the key support for Destroyers. With the ball, Kulwant Khejroliya (2/28 in 4 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DE 156/8 (20 ov) against DES 158/4 (18.2 ov).

The result moves the 2025 series to Destroyers 2–0 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 12 Sep 2025.
