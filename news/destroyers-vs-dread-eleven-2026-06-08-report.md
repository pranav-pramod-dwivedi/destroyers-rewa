# Venkatesh Iyer's 82* (48 balls) fires Destroyers to 7-wicket win in T20 derby

 By **RDCA Sports Desk**
 •
 Match Reporter

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Destroyers won by 7 wickets in a T20 derby at Martand School Ground No. 3, Rewa on 8 Jun 2026. Destroyers won the toss and elected to bowl first.

**Venkatesh Iyer** (Destroyers) took player-of-the-match honours for **82* (48 balls)**. Ajay Rohera (44 off 32) provided the key support for Destroyers. With the ball, Kulwant Khejroliya (3/26 in 4 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DE 154/8 (20 ov) against DES 156/3 (17.5 ov).

The result moves the 2026 series to Destroyers 1–1 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 12 Jun 2026.
