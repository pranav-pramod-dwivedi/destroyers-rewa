# 2021 Breakthrough Battle: Dread Eleven Edge Destroyers 5–2 in Seven T20 Derbies

 By **Vindhya Cricket Archive**
 •
 Senior Analyst

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — The 2021 derby series between Destroyers and Dread Eleven finished 2–5, played across 2021-08-01 to 2021-08-28 in the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament.

Game by game: 1 Aug 2021: Dread Eleven won by 3 wickets (POTM Venkatesh Iyer); 4 Aug 2021: Dread Eleven won by 2 wickets (POTM Amarjeet Kumar Singh); 7 Aug 2021: Destroyers won by 20 runs (POTM Amarjeet Kumar Singh); 11 Aug 2021: Dread Eleven won by 3 wickets (POTM Rohit Rajawat); 15 Aug 2021: Dread Eleven won by 5 wickets (POTM Rajat Patidar); 20 Aug 2021: Dread Eleven won by 2 wickets (POTM Akhil Mishra); 28 Aug 2021: Destroyers won by 14 runs (POTM Pranav Dwivedi).

Serial protagonists: Amarjeet Kumar Singh (2), Venkatesh Iyer (1), Rohit Rajawat (1). The series story in one line — Dread Eleven kicked off the rivalry era with a 5–2 T20 series win over Destroyers in August 2021, setting the tone for Rewa's fiercest derby.
