# Kuldeep Sen's 46 runs off 30 balls steers Dread Eleven to 21-run win in T20 derby

 By **Derby Correspondent**
 •
 Ground Reporter

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Dread Eleven won by 21 runs in a T20 derby at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium, Rewa on 12 Aug 2022. Destroyers won the toss and elected to bat first.

**Kuldeep Sen** (Dread Eleven) took player-of-the-match honours for **46 runs off 30 balls**. Saransh Surana (38 off 25) provided the key support for Dread Eleven. With the ball, Rohit Rajawat (2/9 in 4 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 162/6 (20 ov) against DE 183/10 (20 ov). The decisive passage said it all: ‘Short, wide outside off. Slapped violently through backward point! The ropes take a beating as Dread Eleven seal the historic derby triumph in front of a roaring crowd!’ (19.6, Akhil Mishra vs Kulwant Khejroliya).

The result moves the 2022 series to Destroyers 0–2 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 4 Sep 2022.
