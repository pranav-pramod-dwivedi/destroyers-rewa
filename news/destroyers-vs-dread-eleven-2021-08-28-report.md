# Pranav Dwivedi's 53 runs off 23 balls fires Destroyers to 14-run win in T20 derby

 By **Vindhya Cricket Archive**
 •
 Senior Analyst

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Destroyers won by 14 runs in a T20 derby at Martand School Ground No. 3, Rewa on 28 Aug 2021. Destroyers won the toss and elected to bowl first.

**Pranav Dwivedi** (Destroyers) took player-of-the-match honours for **53 runs off 23 balls**. Mohd Arham Aquil (43 off 28) provided the key support for Destroyers. With the ball, Rishab Verma (1/17 in 4 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 183/10 (20 ov) against DE 169/5 (20 ov). The decisive passage said it all: ‘Short, wide outside off. Slapped violently through backward point! The ropes take a beating as Dread Eleven seal the historic derby triumph in front of a roaring crowd!’ (19.6, Akhil Mishra vs Kulwant Khejroliya).

The result seals the 2021 series 2–5 for Dread Eleven. Played as the 2021 Championship Final.
