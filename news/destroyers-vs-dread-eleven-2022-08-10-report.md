# Derby thriller: Amarjeet Kumar Singh's 3/71 in 4 ov seals it for Dread Eleven

 By **RDCA Media Cell**
 •
 Press Officer

## Tactical Analysis &amp; Match Flow

Rewa, MP — Dread Eleven won by 3 wickets in a T20 derby at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium, Rewa on 10 Aug 2022. Dread Eleven won the toss and elected to bat first.

**Amarjeet Kumar Singh** (Dread Eleven) took player-of-the-match honours for **3/71 in 4 ov**. Naveen Singh Chouhan (53 off 34) provided the key support for Dread Eleven. With the ball, Kumar Kartikeya (2/5 in 3 ov) kept the pressure on.

The game flowed through two contrasting innings: DES 155/9 (20 ov) against DE 173/7 (20 ov). The decisive passage said it all: ‘Short, wide outside off. Slapped violently through backward point! The ropes take a beating as Dread Eleven seal the historic derby triumph in front of a roaring crowd!’ (19.6, Akhil Mishra vs Kulwant Khejroliya).

The result moves the 2022 series to Destroyers 0–1 Dread Eleven, with the sides meeting next on 12 Aug 2022.
