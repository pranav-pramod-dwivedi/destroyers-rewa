Divisional Trials & Academy Scouting Guidelines

Destroyers Cricket Club conducts annual open talent evaluations in collaboration with Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA) certified scouts. Aspirants across Vindhya region are screened through rigorous speed-gun telemetry, net batting against state spinners, and fielding agility benchmarks.

Age Eligibility Categories

Under-19 Development Squad, Under-23 Emerging Warriors, and Senior Franchise Trial Pool.

Mandatory Documentation

RDCA club registration card, government age proof (Aadhaar or birth certificate), and medical fitness clearance.

Kit & Gear Protocol

Standard white flannel or club jersey, spikes for turf wickets, and personal certified safety helmet and pads.

Match Day Stadium Access & Transit Directions

APSU Stadium (Awadhesh Pratap Singh University)

Located on Sirmour Road, Rewa. Accessible via local auto-rickshaw and city buses from Rewa Junction Railway Station (approximately 6.5 km). Dedicated gate entry for general grandstands (Gate 2) and VIP/Press pavilion (Gate 1).

Coordinates: 24.5362° N, 81.3037° E • Parking available at University West Grounds.

Martand School Ground No. 3

Frequently Asked Questions (Trials, Media & Access)

Are match tickets required for Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament games?

Spectator access for Destroyers Cricket Club tournament matches is complimentary across all open grandstand zones in Rewa.

  • General Grandstands: Complimentary open seating at APSU Stadium (Gate 2) and Martand School Ground No. 3 eastern bank.
  • VIP & Player Pavilion: Dedicated pavilion badges required, administered by RDCA and franchise leadership.
  • Derby Climax Access: Capacity crowds of up to 10,000 spectators are accommodated at APSU Stadium on championship final days.

Gates open 60 minutes prior to match commencement with free public parking on campus grounds.

How can local players apply for Destroyers franchise selection trials?

Destroyers Cricket Club conducts structured recruitment drives under the direct supervision of franchise captain Pranav Dwivedi.

  • Online Application: Submit athletic bio, primary playing role, and certified scorecards via the inquiry form above.
  • Assessment Modules: Candidates undergo radar speed-gun assessments, turf net batting trials, and dynamic fielding drills.
  • Age Brackets: Roster opportunities across Under-19 Developmental, Under-23 Emerging Warriors, and Senior First XI pools.

Selected trialists are inducted into Destroyers pre-season training camps at APSU Stadium.

How are media and broadcast credentials issued for the Rewa Derby?

Official press accreditation for Destroyers home matches is issued through the Rewa Division Cricket Association communications wing.

  • Filing Window: Press requests must be logged 48 hours before match toss via email or the online desk.
  • Accreditation Package: Sideline photographer bib, high-speed press lounge WiFi, and post-match interview pool access.
  • Broadcast Rights: Live video recording and streaming requires formal commercial clearance from RDCA officials.

Credential badges can be retrieved from the APSU Stadium Gate 1 accreditation counter on game day.

Tournament Playing Conditions & Regulatory Compliance

Destroyers Cricket Club strictly adheres to the 14 statutory tournament regulations and modern playing amendments codified by the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA). Captain Pranav Dwivedi and team management incorporate key tactical adjustments for complex rule changes:

In-Match Over Rate Penalty

Fielding side must commence the final over by scheduled cutoff. Failure incurs an immediate fielding restriction (only 4 fielders allowed outside the 30-yard circle for all subsequent overs).

60-Second Stop-Clock

Bowlers must be ready within 60 seconds between overs. Two warnings are allowed per innings; a 3rd breach costs a 5-run penalty awarded to the opposition.

15° Bowling Action Scrutiny

Strict compliance with the 15-degree elbow extension limit. Biomechanical 3D motion analysis applies to any reported bowling action under RDCA oversight.

PMOA Electronic Device Blackout

Full communication blackout in dressing rooms from 60 minutes pre-toss until post-match. Zero smartwatches or mobile devices permitted in team areas.
