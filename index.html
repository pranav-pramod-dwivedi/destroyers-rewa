DESTROYERS CLINCH THE 2022 TOURNAMENT TROPHY

In a masterclass of tournament cricket at APSU Stadium, Rewa, Destroyers Cricket Club (DES) defeated arch-rivals DE by 21 runs. Power hitting from Kuldeep Sen (46 off 30) and Saransh Surana (38 off 25) propelled Destroyers to 183/10, before Ashwin Das and Kumar Kartikeya locked down DE's chase to seal the championship.
