Rewa Cricket Tradition

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

Named after former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee, this tournament represents the pinnacle of division-level trial cricket in the Vindhya region under the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA).

The DE vs DES rivalry was forged in 2021 to test elite cricketers across Madhya Pradesh state and regional pools under intense match conditions. With Destroyers (DES) anchored by Akhil Mishra and a lethal pace attack, and DE driven by all-rounder Pranav Dwivedi, these 24 clashes became the premier fixture of Rewa cricket.

Hosted at the legendary Awadhesh Pratap Singh University (APSU) Stadium and Martand School Ground No. 3, these matches continue to define division excellence.
