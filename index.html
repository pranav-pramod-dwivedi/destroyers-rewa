The Rewa Heritage

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Cricket Tournament

Held in honor of former Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee, the tournament in Rewa represents the pinnacle of division-level competitive cricket in the Vindhya region under the aegis of the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA).

The DE vs DES series was established in 2021 as a fierce intra-squad rivalry to test and hone elite talent from Rewa and Madhya Pradesh state pools. Featuring stars from state and marquee setups — including Pranav Dwivedi, Akhil Mishra, Rajat Patidar, Venkatesh Iyer, Avesh Khan, Kuldeep Sen, and Kumar Kartikeya — this clash became synonymous with unyielding passion and tactical precision.

Matches are hosted primarily at the iconic Awadhesh Pratap Singh University Stadium and Martand School Ground No. 3 in Rewa, drawing local cricket fans and division scouts alike.
