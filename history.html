The Crowning Glory

The 2022 Championship Final

How Destroyers Cricket Club conquered DE by 21 runs on 12 August 2022 at Awadhesh Pratap Singh University Stadium to hoist the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Trophy.

Final Match Protocol • 12 Aug 2022

DESTROYERS 183 DEF DE 162/6 (BY 21 RUNS)

Batting first in high-pressure championship conditions, Destroyers launched an aggressive counter-attack after early turbulence. Kuldeep Sen produced a brutal 46 off just 30 deliveries featuring 4 fours and 3 sixes, while Saransh Surana chipped in with an electrifying 38 off 25. Destroyers posted 183 all out in 19.3 overs.

Defending 183, Destroyers' bowling unit demonstrated icy discipline. Despite a valiant 68 from DE talisman Pranav Dwivedi, strike spells from Ashwin Das and spin strangulation from Kumar Kartikeya restricted DE to 162/6, delivering the trophy to Destroyers.

The Vindhya Crucible

The Atal Bihari Vajpayee Memorial Tournament

Named in honor of former Prime Minister of India, Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee, this premier tournament serves as the high-stakes evaluation arena for the Rewa Division Cricket Association (RDCA), affiliated with the Madhya Pradesh Cricket Association (MPCA) and the BCCI.

The intra-squad rivalry between Destroyers Cricket Club (DES) and DE was inaugurated in August 2021 to test top-tier talent in match-condition pressure cookers. Over 4 consecutive seasons (2021–2024), 24 fixtures were contested across 20-over T20 and 50-over ODI formats.

The series has featured Ranji Trophy stars, IPL talents, and regional warriors, maintaining a relentless standard of sportsmanship, tactical intensity, and competitive fire in the heart of Rewa.
